Insights

Questions worth asking early.

General information only. Legal advice should be based on the particular facts, documents and applicable law of your matter.

Commercial

Contracts should answer the difficult questions early.

Payment, performance, risk allocation, termination, confidentiality and dispute mechanisms deserve attention before a commercial relationship becomes strained.

Business

Structure should reflect how the business actually works.

Ownership, control, responsibilities and risk should be considered alongside registration and paperwork.

Litigation

Preserve the record before a dispute escalates.

Contracts, messages, receipts, notices and timelines may become important if negotiation develops into formal proceedings.

Family

Sensitive matters still need clear documentation.

Family disputes often involve urgent personal decisions alongside legal procedure. Early advice can help identify what documents and orders may be relevant.

Privacy

Know the personal data your organisation holds.

Privacy compliance starts with understanding what data is collected, the reason for collection, how it is secured and who receives it.

General

Good legal advice begins with the actual decision.

Bring the relevant documents, dates and desired outcome into focus before choosing a legal route.
