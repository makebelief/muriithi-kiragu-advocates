About the firm

Practical legal support from Nakuru.

A boutique practice built around clear advice, direct access to counsel and focused legal action across business, property and personal matters.

The practice

Counsel shaped around the decision in front of you.

Muriithi S. Kiragu & Co. Advocates operates from Longonot House in Nakuru. The firm’s published service profile covers civil litigation, commercial and contract law, conveyancing and property, succession and probate, employment and labour relations, family law, criminal defence, and data protection and privacy law.

The firm is presented as a boutique practice: clients have a direct point of contact and legal work is approached with attention to the practical consequences surrounding the matter.
